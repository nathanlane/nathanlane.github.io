This research investigates the role of digital transformation in enhancing manufacturing firm resilience, particularly in response to global supply chain disruptions. Using firm-level data from multiple countries, we examine how adoption of Industry 4.0 technologies affects firms' ability to adapt to external shocks.

## Research Questions

- How does digital technology adoption affect manufacturing firms' resilience to supply chain disruptions?
- What are the key mechanisms through which digitalization enhances adaptive capacity?
- Do these effects vary by firm size, industry, or geographic location?

## Methodology

We employ a difference-in-differences approach, exploiting variation in the timing of digital technology adoption across firms and regions. Our identification strategy leverages exogenous shocks to global supply chains during 2020-2023.

## Preliminary Findings

Initial results suggest that firms with higher levels of digital integration showed greater resilience during supply chain disruptions, with effects particularly pronounced for:
- Real-time inventory management systems
- Automated production scheduling
- Digital supplier networks

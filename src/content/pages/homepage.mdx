I'm a economics professor and empirical economist working at the intersection of big data, economic development, political economy, and industrial economics. Find my [research here](/research/).

My work has appeared in [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2023/09/08/opinion/industrial-policy-biden.html), [The Economist](https://www.economist.com/special-report/2023/10/02/governments-across-the-world-are-discovering-homeland-economics), [The Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/34872d9a-3587-4b27-a01d-2905f8e23408), [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/articles/this-part-of-bidenomics-needs-more-economics-2cea1641?ns=prod%2Faccounts-wsj), [Noahpinion](https://www.noahpinion.blog/p/a-few-economists-are-starting-to), [Trade Talks](https://tradetalkspodcast.com/podcast/189-south-koreas-controversial-industrial-policy/), and [more](/media/).

See my work with The [Industrial Policy Group](https://www.industrialpolicygroup.com/), an empirical research lab. I'm an affiliate of CESifo research network and a cofounder of [Sodalabs.io](https://sodalabs.io).

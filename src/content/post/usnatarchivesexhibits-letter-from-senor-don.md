<p><em>Item From: General Records of the Department of State. (09/1789-)<br /></em></p>
<p>This letter, written by the Spanish Ambassador to the United States, criticized President McKinley by calling him weak. The publication of the letter caused the public to support a war with Spain over the independence of the colony of Cuba.</p>
